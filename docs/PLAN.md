# 🗺️ Sleepy Space Explorer — Channel Plan

> Created: 2026-04-23 | Status: Phase 1 in progress

## Concept

- **Channel name:** Sleepy Space Explorer
- **Format:** 30-minute sleep stories for kids, narrated by AI voice
- **Distribution:** YouTube (primary), then Spotify/Podcasts
- **Niche:** Space-themed bedtime stories (cosmos, planets, astronauts, stars)
- **Target age:** 5–10 years
- **Monetization:** YouTube AdSense → Patreon/Ko-fi (ad-free tier) → merch

---

## Production Pipeline (6 steps)

1. ✍️ **Write** — draft story script (4,500–5,000 words for 30 min at 0.80x)
2. 🎙️ **Voice** — OpenAI TTS, voice: shimmer, speed: 0.80
3. 🎨 **Art** — DALL·E or Midjourney cover image (1280×720 or square for thumbnail)
4. 🎵 **Audio** — mix narration + ambient background (soft rain, space hum) in Audacity
5. 🎬 **Video** — static image loop + audio → export via CapCut or ffmpeg
6. 📤 **Publish** — YouTube upload with optimized title, description, tags
