# Episode 01 — Script
## A Gentle Journey to the Moon 🌙

> **Voice:** Shimmer | **Speed:** 0.80x | **Target length:** ~30 min (4,500–5,000 words)

---

### INTRO

*[Soft ambient space hum begins — barely audible, like distant stars]*

Hello, little explorer...

I'm so glad you're here tonight.

It's time to close your eyes... take one slow, deep breath in...

...and let it go.

Good.

Tonight, we're going somewhere very special.

Somewhere quiet... somewhere soft... somewhere that glows just for you.

Tonight... we're going to the moon.

---

### PART 1 — LIFTING OFF

Find yourself lying in your bed... so comfortable... so warm.

Imagine you're wearing the coziest spacesuit you've ever seen. It's soft on the inside — like wearing a cloud. Your helmet is clear so you can see everything, and it smells just a little bit like warm vanilla.

You take another slow breath in...

...and you feel yourself becoming lighter.

Just a little.

Your blanket is still around you. Your pillow is still soft beneath your head. But your body... your body is beginning to float.

Gently. Slowly. Like a feather lifted by the softest breeze.

You rise... just a little...

...and you begin to drift upward.

Through your ceiling — which isn't there anymore. Just open, warm air.

The houses below you grow smaller. The lights of your neighborhood flicker like little fireflies in the dark.

You aren't afraid.

You are safe.

You are held, gently, by the sky itself.

---

### PART 2 — THROUGH THE CLOUDS

As you float higher, you drift into a cloud.

It's soft. Like cotton. Like the fluffiest blanket you've ever touched.

The cloud wraps around you — cool and gentle — and for a moment, everything is white and still.

You breathe in...

The air smells like rain and something sweet, like the air just before a storm that never quite comes.

And then... you float through.

And above you...

The sky is dark.

But not a scary dark.

A quiet dark.

A soft dark, full of stars.

Each one a tiny light, placed there just for you.

You count them slowly in your mind...

One...

Two...

Three...

*[Pause — long]*

You don't need to count very high before your eyes feel heavy.

That's okay. Let them be heavy.

You're still floating. Still safe. Still drifting gently toward the moon.

---

### PART 3 — DEEP SPACE

The Earth below you is round and blue now.

You can see the swirls of clouds, the patches of land, the great wide oceans glinting softly.

It is beautiful.

Everything you love is down there — safe and warm and waiting for you when you wake.

But right now... right now you are here.

In the quiet.

In the deep, soft quiet of space.

There are no sounds here except your breathing.

In...

...and out.

In...

...and out.

The stars don't flicker up here. They shine steady and still.

You reach out a hand — just barely — and one tiny star drifts close to your fingertip.

It's warm.

You smile.

It pulses once — like a heartbeat — and then drifts back.

The universe knows you're here.

And it is glad.

---

### PART 4 — APPROACHING THE MOON

Ahead of you, growing larger and larger...

The moon.

It glows.

Not sharp or bright — but soft. Like a nightlight left on just for you.

The surface is gentle hills and quiet craters, covered in silver dust that catches the light.

You drift closer.

Closer.

Your spacesuit is still warm. Still soft.

You take a slow breath in...

And you realize — you're not tired because anything is wrong.

You're tired because you've had a full day.

A wonderful, full day.

And now... now it is time for rest.

---

### PART 5 — LANDING ON THE MOON

Your feet touch down — so gently — on the soft silver dust.

It puffs up in slow, lazy clouds around your boots.

Each step here is a slow bounce.

Slow...

Quiet...

You find a small, smooth hill and you sit down on it.

The dust is soft beneath you.

The Earth hangs in the sky above — blue and white and glowing.

You look at it.

You think of your bed. Your pillow. Your people.

And you feel full.

Not sad — full. Warm. Complete.

The moon is quiet around you.

There is nothing to do here.

Nothing to worry about.

Nothing to fix or figure out.

Just... this.

The stillness.

The stars.

The soft glow.

And you.

---

### WIND-DOWN

Your eyes are very heavy now.

That's okay.

Let them close.

You are still on your moon hill. Still warm. Still safe.

Breathe in slowly...

...and breathe out slowly.

The Earth turns gently above you.

The stars hold still.

The moon dust is soft and warm beneath you.

You don't have to go anywhere.

You don't have to do anything.

You just...

...rest.

Your arms are heavy.

Your legs are heavy.

Your whole body is sinking — softly — into warmth.

Let it.

You are safe, little explorer.

You are loved.

You are home — wherever you are.

Breathe in...

...and out.

In...

...and out.

The moon keeps watch tonight.

The stars are all around you.

And you...

...you are drifting...

...gently...

...gently...

...to sleep.

*[Ambient fades to near-silence]*

*[End]*
