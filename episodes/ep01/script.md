# Episode 01 — Script
## A Gentle Journey to the Moon 🌙

> **Voice:** Fable (OpenAI TTS) | **Speed:** 1.0x | **Pacing:** ellipses + line breaks | **Target length:** ~30 min (4,500–5,000 words)

---

### INTRO

Hello...

little explorer.

I'm so glad you're here tonight.

It's time to rest now.

Whatever happened today...

it's done.

You don't need to think about it anymore.

Right now...

all you need to do...

is breathe.

Take one slow breath in...

...hold it gently...

...and let it go.

Good.

One more.

In...

...and out.

Your body knows how to do this.

It's been doing it your whole life.

In...

...and out.

Tonight...

we're going somewhere very special.

Somewhere quiet.

Somewhere soft.

Somewhere that glows...

just for you.

Tonight...

we're going to the moon.

---

### PART 1 — SETTLING IN

Before we lift off...

let's make sure you're completely comfortable.

Feel your pillow beneath your head.

It's the perfect softness, isn't it.

And your blanket...

feel how warm it is.

How it wraps around you...

like a hug that doesn't let go.

Good.

Now let your body sink...

just a little heavier...

into your mattress.

Feel the weight of your arms.

They're heavy tonight.

That's okay.

They've worked hard today.

Let them be heavy.

Let them rest.

Feel the weight of your legs.

Heavy too.

That's okay.

They've carried you everywhere today.

Let them rest now.

And your eyes...

your beautiful, tired eyes...

let them be heavy too.

You don't need to see anything right now.

Everything you need...

is already here.

---

### PART 2 — LIFTING OFF

Now...

imagine your spacesuit.

It appears around you...

soft and warm...

like it was made just for your body.

The inside is lined with something cozy...

like the softest fleece you've ever touched.

Your helmet appears too...

clear and clean...

and it smells just faintly...

of something warm.

Maybe vanilla.

Maybe a little like bedtime.

You take a slow breath in...

and you feel something happen.

Your body...

becomes lighter.

Just a little.

Your blanket is still around you.

Your pillow is still soft beneath your head.

But your body...

is beginning to float.

Gently.

Slowly.

Like a feather...

lifted by the softest breeze you've ever felt.

You rise...

just a little...

and begin to drift...

upward.

Through the ceiling...

which simply isn't there anymore.

Just warm, open air.

The roof of your home drifts below you.

Then the treetops.

Then the rooftops of all the houses on your street...

each one with a warm light in the window.

Families inside.

Everyone resting.

Everyone safe.

Including you.

You float higher.

The lights below you...

flicker like fireflies in the dark.

They are beautiful.

You are not afraid.

You are held...

gently...

by the sky itself.

---

### PART 3 — THROUGH THE CLOUDS

Higher now.

The air around you grows cooler...

and softer.

And then...

you drift into a cloud.

Oh.

It is soft.

Like cotton.

Like the fluffiest blanket you've ever touched.

The cloud wraps around you...

cool and gentle...

and for a moment...

everything is white.

And still.

And quiet.

You breathe in.

The air smells like rain...

and something sweet.

Like the air right before a summer storm...

that never quite comes.

You breathe out.

And then...

slowly...

you float through.

And above you...

the sky opens.

It is dark.

But not a scary dark.

A quiet dark.

A soft, deep dark...

full of stars.

So many stars.

Each one a tiny light...

placed there...

just for you.

You begin to count them...

very slowly...

in your mind.

One...

Two...

Three...

Four...

You don't need to count very high.

Before long...

your eyes feel heavy.

That's okay.

Let them be heavy.

You're still floating.

Still safe.

Still drifting...

gently...

toward the moon.

---

### PART 4 — DEEP SPACE

The Earth below you...

is round now.

Blue and white and glowing.

You can see the great swirls of clouds...

like brushstrokes on a painting.

The deep blue of the oceans.

The soft browns and greens of the land.

It is so beautiful.

Everything you love is down there.

Safe.

Warm.

Waiting for you...

when you wake.

But right now...

right now you are here.

In the quiet.

In the deep...

soft...

quiet of space.

There are no sounds here.

Only your breathing.

In...

...and out.

In...

...and out.

The stars don't flicker up here.

They shine steady.

And still.

You reach out one hand...

slowly...

and a tiny star drifts close to your fingertip.

It is warm.

You smile.

It pulses once...

like a heartbeat...

and drifts back.

The universe knows you're here.

And it is glad.

---

### PART 5 — APPROACHING THE MOON

Ahead of you...

growing larger...

and larger...

the moon.

It glows.

Not sharp or bright.

Soft.

Like a nightlight...

left on...

just for you.

The surface is smooth rolling hills...

and gentle craters...

covered in silver dust...

that catches the light.

You drift closer.

Closer.

Your spacesuit is still warm.

Your breathing...

is slow.

In...

...and out.

You realize something.

You're not tired because anything is wrong.

You're tired...

because you've had a full day.

A real day.

A day full of things.

And now...

it is time to rest.

---

### PART 6 — LANDING

Your feet touch down.

So gently.

The silver dust puffs up around your boots...

in slow, lazy little clouds.

Each step here...

is a slow bounce.

Quiet.

Gentle.

You find a small round hill...

and you sit down on it.

The dust is soft beneath you.

The Earth hangs in the sky above...

blue and white and glowing.

You look at it.

You think of your bed.

Your pillow.

Your people.

And you feel...

full.

Not sad.

Full.

Warm.

Complete.

The moon is quiet around you.

There is nothing to do here.

Nothing to worry about.

Nothing to figure out.

Just...

this.

The stillness.

The stars.

The soft glow all around you.

And you.

---

Your eyes are very heavy now.

That's okay.

Let them close.

You are still on your moon hill.

Still warm.

Still safe.

Breathe in...

slowly...

...and breathe out...

slowly.

In...

...out.

The Earth turns gently above you.

The stars hold still.

The moon dust is soft and warm beneath you.

You don't have to go anywhere.

You don't have to do anything.

You just...

...rest.

Your fingers are relaxed.

Your arms are heavy.

Your legs are heavy.

Your whole body...

is sinking...

softly...

into warmth.

Let it.

You are safe...

little explorer.

You are loved.

You are exactly where you're supposed to be.

Breathe in...

...and out.

In...

...and out.

The moon keeps watch tonight.

The stars are all around you.

And you...

...are drifting...

...gently...

...gently...

...to sleep.
