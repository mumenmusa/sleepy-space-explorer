# Episode 02 — Floating Through the Rings of Saturn
# Target: ~20 min | ~22,000 chars | 20 chunks | Fable 1.0x

---

The night is quiet.

...

The kind of quiet that comes after everyone has gone to sleep...

and the house has settled...

and the only sound left in the world...

is the wind moving very gently through the eucalyptus tree.

...

You're outside.

...

The grass is cool beneath your feet.

The air smells like the end of the day —

a little warm still...

from the sun that was here just a few hours ago...

and a little sharp...

that eucalyptus smell...

clean and soft at the same time.

...

The orange tree stands at the corner of the yard.

The fruit hangs round and heavy...

glowing faintly in the moonlight...

like small lanterns someone forgot to take inside.

...

The chickens are quiet in their coop.

You can see the warm glow from their little window.

Just a soft orange light...

tucked behind the wooden slats...

peaceful.

...

You breathe in.

...

You breathe out.

...

The hills beyond the neighborhood are dark shapes against the sky.

No lights on them.

Just the shapes...

rolling and still.

...

The plumeria by the gate smells faint and sweet...

a smell that only comes out at night.

...

You stand there for a moment.

Just standing.

Just breathing.

...

Above you, the sky is enormous.

...

So many stars.

More than you can count...

more than anyone could ever count...

and they're all just...

there.

Waiting.

Shining.

...

You tilt your head back and look up.

...

And that's when you see it.

---

...

...

...

It's there.

...

The little silver spacecraft...

sitting right where it always is...

at the edge of the yard...

near the orange tree.

...

Round and quiet.

Barely bigger than a large car.

...

And humming.

...

Not loud.

Just a low, gentle hum...

like a sleeping thing...

like something alive and patient...

like it has been waiting here...

all day...

just for this moment.

...

You walk toward it slowly.

The grass whispers under your feet.

...

A monarch butterfly lifts from the milkweed nearby —

you can just barely see it in the dark...

orange and black wings opening once...

very slowly...

then drifting away into the night.

...

You watch it go.

...

Then you turn back to the ship.

...

The door slides open.

Soft warm light spills out onto the grass.

...

You duck inside.

...

The seat is just right.

Soft padding...

the shape of it fitting you perfectly...

like it was made just for you.

...

You settle in.

...

Your shoulders drop.

Your hands rest in your lap.

...

The door closes...

quietly...

with a soft click.

---

...

...

The hum gets a little warmer.

...

A little deeper.

...

You feel it more than hear it —

a gentle vibration...

moving up through the seat...

through your back...

through your shoulders...

into your jaw.

...

Not uncomfortable.

Just...

alive.

...

And then...

very slowly...

very gently...

the ground falls away.

...

You rise.

...

The orange tree shrinks below you.

The roof of the house gets smaller.

The mishmish tree beside the fence...

the olive tree in the corner...

the wide dark spread of the yard...

it all begins to shrink.

...

The neighborhood becomes a patchwork...

little squares of gold light...

little dark streets between them.

...

And up above...

nothing but stars.

...

You lean back in your seat.

...

You're not scared.

You were never scared.

...

The lights of the neighborhood are gold and orange below you now.

Tiny.

Like the embers of something.

...

Somewhere down there...

everyone is asleep.

...

This is the best feeling in the world —

rising into the dark...

slow and steady...

carried.

---

...

...

The clouds come first.

...

They always come first.

...

They're lit from below by the city lights —

pale gold at the edges...

deep grey in the middle...

and then you're inside one.

...

Everything goes soft.

Mist on the window.

The world outside turns white...

then pale...

then...

you're through.

...

Above the clouds now.

...

The stars are sharper up here.

No blurry edges.

No shimmer.

Just clean, cold points of light...

each one steady...

each one its own sun...

far, far away.

...

Below you, the cloud layer spreads out in every direction.

Like a white blanket...

covering everything...

soft and thick and still.

...

You press your hand to the glass.

...

The window is cool.

...

You watch the clouds until they disappear in the dark below.

...

And now it's just...

space.

...

Just the stars.

And the hum of the ship.

And you.

---

...

...

The moon is off to the left.

...

It hangs there, round and silver...

enormous from this distance.

...

You can see its craters from here.

Wide shallow circles...

shadows pooled at their edges...

each one a record of something that happened...

a very long time ago.

...

The moon looks the same as it always has from Earth —

round and grey and glowing —

but from up here...

you notice things.

The shadow on one side.

The way the light catches the ridges.

The way the surface is a thousand different shades of grey...

not just one.

...

You press your nose to the window for just a moment.

...

Then lean back.

...

The ship carries you onward.

...

Past the moon.

...

Further.

...

The sun is behind you now —

a bright white light at the edge of your vision —

smaller than it looks from home...

but still warm...

still steady...

still there.

...

You look forward.

Into the dark.

...

Somewhere out there...

a long way from here...

is where you're going.

---

...

...

After a while...

long after the moon has faded behind you...

long after the Earth has shrunk to just a pale blue dot...

things begin to drift past the window.

...

Rocks.

...

Not sharp or scary rocks.

Just...

floating rocks.

...

Some of them are small —

like pebbles spinning slowly in the dark.

Some of them are bigger —

wide and rounded and grey...

tumbling end over end...

very slowly.

One here.

Then a gap of empty space.

Then two more.

...

This is the asteroid belt.

The wide ring between the inner planets and the outer ones.

A long, slow scattering of leftover pieces...

from when the solar system was still being made...

a very, very long time ago.

...

The ship weaves between them...

quietly...

smoothly...

like drifting through a slow, wide river.

...

You watch the rocks drift past.

Each one different.

Some pale grey.

Some darker, almost black.

One with a bright stripe of something lighter running across it...

like a scar.

...

One catches the sunlight just right...

and glimmers gold for a moment...

and then it's past.

Gone into the dark.

...

You keep going.

---

...

...

A shape appears ahead.

...

At first you think it's just a bright star.

But it doesn't flicker.

And it's getting bigger.

...

Slowly...

slowly...

it becomes something round.

...

Striped.

...

Orange and cream and pale brown...

bands of cloud wrapping all the way around it...

like the patterns you'd find in old polished stone...

but moving.

...

Very slowly moving.

...

Jupiter.

...

The biggest planet.

...

If the sun were a front door...

Jupiter would be about the size of a basketball...

and Earth would be about the size of a pea...

sitting somewhere down the block.

...

You feel the ship get very small next to it.

You feel yourself get very small next to it.

...

But not in a frightening way.

In the way that feels like wonder.

Like standing at the edge of the ocean and looking out...

and knowing the water goes all the way to somewhere you've never been.

...

You stay with it for a moment.

Just looking.

...

Four moons orbit Jupiter.

Faint points of light arranged in a line on either side.

Each one its own world.

Each one going around and around...

year after year...

century after century...

quiet and steady.

...

There are storms on Jupiter —

storms larger than the whole Earth —

but from here...

from the window of your little ship...

you can only see the bands of cloud...

slow and wide and ancient...

moving in opposite directions at once.

...

You pass by...

quiet...

watching.

...

Jupiter falls behind.

---

...

...

Now you are very far from home.

...

The sun behind you is smaller.

Still bright...

still the brightest thing out here...

but smaller than you've ever seen it.

Like someone turned the dimmer down.

...

The stars don't change.

They never change.

From any point in the solar system...

the stars look the same.

...

That's something worth thinking about.

...

Wherever you go...

the stars are the same stars.

The same ones your grandparents looked up at.

The same ones that were there before there were any people at all.

The same ones that will still be there...

long, long after.

...

They make the dark feel...

friendly.

...

You can feel your own breathing now.

In.

...

Out.

...

The seat holds you.

The ship carries you.

The stars surround you.

...

You are very small out here.

And somehow...

that feels fine.

...

You lean back in your seat.

Your arms are heavy.

Your eyes want to drift.

...

But look —

...

something is up ahead.

...

Something pale.

Something glowing.

...

Something ringed.

---

...

...

There it is.

...

Saturn.

...

You stay very still.

...

You don't want to move.

You don't want to do anything that might make this feel less real.

...

You've seen pictures.

You know what it looks like.

But pictures don't prepare you for this.

...

It's pale gold.

Almost cream.

A soft, glowing ball...

hanging in the black...

and around it...

the rings.

...

They stretch out on both sides...

flat and wide and perfectly even...

like someone drew a single clean line through the middle of a planet...

except the line is made of millions and millions of pieces of ice...

and dust...

and broken rock...

each one on its own path...

each one orbiting...

all of them together...

in perfect silence.

...

You can't hear anything.

Space is silent.

But if you could hear the rings...

you imagine it would sound like a river.

...

A very slow, very wide, very quiet river.

...

You watch it get bigger as the ship moves closer.

...

Closer.

...

Closer.

---

...

...

The ship angles toward the edge of the rings.

...

You hold your breath.

...

And then you're in.

...

All around you...

on every side...

ice.

...

Pieces of it —

some the size of a fingernail...

some the size of a house —

floating past the window.

...

Each one catching the faraway sunlight...

and throwing it back...

in tiny flashes...

silver...

white...

pale blue.

...

It's like being inside a snow globe.

...

The kind someone shook gently once...

and then set down...

and the flakes never stopped falling.

...

The ship moves slowly.

...

There's no rush here.

No reason to hurry.

...

You watch the pieces drift past.

Each one on its own path.

Each one making a slow, endless circle around the planet.

...

They've been doing this for billions of years.

...

Long before there were people.

Long before there were chickens, or orange trees, or backyards, or anyone at all to look up and wonder.

...

They were just...

here.

Going around.

Quiet.

---

...

...

Through the rings...

past the drifting ice...

Saturn fills more and more of the window.

...

It's not quite like any color you've seen before.

...

Not yellow.

Not orange.

Something in between.

Warm.

...

Like afternoon light on pale stone.

Like the color of honey held up to a lamp.

...

Bands of slightly different shades run across it...

wide and slow...

gentle gradients of cream and gold...

and here and there...

a faint streak of something darker.

...

There are storms on Saturn too...

but they look nothing like storms from here.

From here they look like brushstrokes.

Like someone painted the whole planet...

with a wide, slow brush...

and then stepped back...

and waited for it to dry.

...

You watch the colors shift.

...

Very slowly.

...

Almost imperceptibly.

...

Like watching clouds move when you're lying in the grass.

...

If you stare at one spot long enough...

nothing seems to change.

But then you look away...

and when you look back...

it has.

---

...

...

The ship rises now.

...

Up and over the rings...

so you can see them from above.

...

From up here...

they look different.

...

Thin.

...

So thin.

...

Hundreds of thousands of kilometers wide...

but only a few hundred meters thick.

...

Like a coin.

...

Like the world's largest, most perfect coin...

spinning slowly around a glowing amber planet.

...

You lean your head against the window.

...

The glass is cool against your cheek.

...

The rings stretch out below you in a great wide arc...

curving away in both directions...

vanishing into the dark on each side.

...

You try to find the edge of them.

You look left.

They go...

and go...

and go.

...

You look right.

The same.

...

There is no edge you can see.

Just the slow, endless curve of them...

bending away into the distance...

like the edge of the world from a very high place.

...

You close your eyes for a moment.

...

You open them again.

...

The rings are still there.

...

There is so much space out here.

...

So much silence.

...

So much...

stillness.

...

Your eyes are heavy.

...

Not from sadness.

Not from boredom.

...

From peace.

---

...

...

Off to the side...

you notice something small.

...

A moon.

...

One of Saturn's many moons.

...

This one is pale and round...

a little smaller than our own moon...

and its surface is smooth...

almost glassy.

...

It's called Enceladus.

...

At its south pole...

beneath a thick shell of ice...

there is a liquid ocean.

Kept warm by the pull of Saturn's gravity...

stretching and squeezing the moon...

very gently...

like kneading bread dough.

...

And from cracks in the ice...

water shoots up into space in long, thin plumes.

Frozen almost instantly by the cold.

...

You watch for one of those plumes as the ship drifts closer.

...

And there —

a faint spray...

catching the distant sunlight...

white...

like the finest mist...

rising and curving and drifting away into the rings.

...

Something is alive down there.

...

Maybe.

...

No one knows for certain.

...

But maybe.

...

You hold that thought gently.

Like something fragile.

Like a soap bubble.

...

You don't want to squeeze it.

Just...

hold it.

---

...

...

The ship settles into a slow drift...

just above the rings.

...

Moving with them now.

...

At the same speed.

...

So the rings look almost still.

...

Just the ice pieces nearby drifting past very slowly...

in no hurry...

going nowhere except around and around.

...

The sunlight out here is softer.

Farther from the sun now...

the light is gentler...

like late afternoon in autumn...

not bright and sharp...

just...

there.

Warm.

Golden.

Enough.

...

You breathe in.

...

Out.

...

The ship hums.

...

The ice drifts.

...

Saturn glows.

...

A very small piece of ice taps the window.

So lightly you almost miss it.

A tiny soft tick.

And then it's past.

...

You watch where it went.

You can't find it anymore.

Just more ice.

All of it the same.

All of it different.

...

Your hands are loose in your lap.

Your shoulders have dropped all the way down.

...

You could stay here for a very long time.

You could stay here...

and just...

watch.

---

...

...

The ship turns slowly...

moving around to the dark side of Saturn.

...

The side facing away from the sun.

...

And here...

something extraordinary happens.

...

From the dark side...

you can see the rings lit from behind.

...

The sun is on the other side of the planet now —

hidden —

...

They glow from within.

...

Not bright.

Not sharp.

...

Saturn itself is a silhouette now.

A dark shape...

...

You don't say anything.

...

There's nothing to say.

...

You just...

watch.

...

And watch.

...

And watch.

...

The dark side of Saturn is a different kind of dark.

Not cold.

...

Just quiet.

When just being there is enough.

...

You breathe slowly.

...

The rings glow.

...

You breathe.

---

...

...

The ship finds a spot.

...

Not on Saturn — Saturn has no surface to land on.

Just clouds...

all the way down.

...

But there is a flat place on one of the smaller moons nearby.

...

A wide, smooth plain of ice.

...

The ship descends...

slowly...

so slowly...

...

One moment you were floating...

the next moment...

still.

...

The hum drops to almost nothing.

...

Through the window...

Saturn hangs enormous in the black sky.

Rings tilted slightly...

glowing...

going around and around...

silent.

...

Below the ship...

the ice is pale blue-white.

...

Not cold-feeling from in here.

Not harsh.

...

Just...

still.

...

Like the whole world out here is resting.

Like it has been resting for a very long time.

Like it knows how.

...

And maybe...

you do too.

...

...

There's a small window in the roof of the ship.

...

You look up through it.

...

Saturn fills most of the sky.

...

The rings arc across from one horizon to the other.

...

...

The light is soft.

...

The silence is complete.

...

...

...

...

be here.

...

...

Your arms rest against the soft padding of the seat.

...

...

Slowly.

...

Around.

...

Around.

...

...
